# Nusa Raya — Dari Nol Menjadi Pahlawan

[![make.fun bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-best--game--01-teal)](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) [![license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue)](LICENSE)

A pixel-art grand-strategy-lite set in a fictional Nusantara archipelago. You are **Wira Nusa**, a landless commander with eight followers and one sword. Each season you move across the campaign map, recruit troops, collect tribute, scheme and parley with four rival rulers — gifts, marriage alliances, tribute demands, sabotage, spies, sowing discord — and when steel is drawn you drop into a real-time top-down battle and fight in the crush yourself, WorldBox style.

Unite all five islands, by conquest or by alliance, within 16 seasons to win.

## How to play

- **Campaign map (per season):** click a neighbouring territory to move your banner, recruit troops, collect tribute, open diplomacy with a ruler, infiltrate a rival, then end the season.
- **Diplomacy:** gifts raise opinion, marriage forges an alliance, tribute demands pay out when you are stronger, infiltration sabotages garrisons, spies reveal armies, sowing discord turns rivals on each other.
- **Battle (real-time, top-down):** WASD / arrows move your hero, mouse aims, click to slash, Space blows the war horn and rallies your troops. Swordsmen beat cavalry, archers beat swordsmen, cavalry beat archers. Break the enemy's morale and they rout.
- **Hero:** earn XP from battles, diplomacy and events; level up and pick 1 of 3 traits. P pauses, M mutes.

## Tech

Plain static files, no build step, no network requests. All art is drawn in code on canvas, all sound is synthesised with WebAudio. Serve with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080.
